See a breakdown of who’s spending to win in Chicago Board of Education districts

Millions of dollars are being spent in key battlegrounds for seats on Chicago’s first fully elected school board. See where special interest groups are spending by clicking or using your mouse on the map below.

Each group’s bars include its ads and its cash and in-kind donations to candidates. SEIU’s spending is shown in two forms: ads paid for by Brighter Futures Chicago, a committee funded entirely by SEIU, and SEIU committees’ cash donations to campaigns. Campaigns’ own spending, in gray, may include money from those donations.
