Chicago Teachers Union is outspent by anti-CTU groups in races around Chicago

Groups like Common Ground Collective and INCS Action are spending heavily to defeat CTU-backed candidates. For incumbents like Norma Rios-Sierra and Anusha Thotakura, special interest groups are outspending CTU by more than 10 to 1. For the most lopsided race, for incumbent Emma Lozano’s seat, the CTU is being outspent by about 25 to 1.

CTU and the affiliated political action committee Our Schools PAC structure most of their support as in-kind help to candidate committees. Common Ground and INCS rely mostly on independent expenditure committees, which are barred from coordinating with campaigns. Other groups employ both strategies.
