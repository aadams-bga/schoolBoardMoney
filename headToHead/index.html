Chicago Teachers Union is outspent by anti-CTU groups in races around Chicago

Groups like Common Ground Collective and INCS Action are spending heavily to defeat CTU-backed candidates. For incumbents like Norma Rios Sierra and Ed Bannon, special interest groups are outspending CTU by roughly 10-to-1 or more. For the most lopsided race, for incumbent Emma Lozano’s seat, the CTU is being outspent by about 27-to-1.
